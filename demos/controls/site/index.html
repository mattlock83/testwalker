Web controls lab

Small, repeatable scenarios for common web interactions. Each control shows its current result and records browser events. All data and attachments are synthetic.

CheckboxesCheck, uncheck and switch notifications. Radio choicesChoose one delivery service. Selection listsSingle, multiple and disabled options. Native dateISO date entry and min/max validation. Calendar date pickerOpen a custom calendar, change month and choose a day. AttachmentsUpload, replace and clear named fixture files. SlidersNative range and accessible keyboard slider. Colour pickerSet a native colour value and observe change events. Drag and dropMove a checklist between two lists. Custom dropdownAccessible options without native select markup. Registration formRequired name and email, acceptance and rejection.
